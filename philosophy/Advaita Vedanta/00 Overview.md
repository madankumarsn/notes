# 00 — Overview: The Advaita Vedanta Sequence

*Sources: Swami Sarvapriyananda — The Atman · Maya · Purnam · Mandukya Upanishad Parts 1 & 2*
*Tradition: Advaita Vedanta (non-dual idealism) — Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, Gaudapada, Shankara*

---

## The central question

**"What am I?"** — not *who* (personality, role, history), but *what* (my essential nature).

Advaita's answer, in one line: **You are pure awareness, and that awareness is the one reality appearing as everything.**

These notes build that answer step by step. Each step uses only what earlier steps established.

### How the steps connect

```
Step 1  separates awareness from objects (within one moment)
   ↓
Step 2  shows awareness continues across waking, dream, deep sleep
   ↓
Step 3  derives what such awareness must be: eternal, real, all-pervading...
   ↓
Step 4  explains why we miss it: ignorance → error; and how knowledge removes it
   ↓
Step 5  closes the gap left in Step 1: objects are not separate from awareness
   ↓
Step 6  names the appearance itself — Maya — and shows it in daily life
```

## A tool used throughout: two levels of truth

| Level | Sanskrit | What is treated as real |
|---|---|---|
| Empirical | *vyavaharika* | World, bodies, karma, rebirth, teacher and student |
| Absolute | *paramarthika* | Only Brahman/Atman; nothing born, nothing dies |

**Example:** "You will be reborn" (empirical) and "you were never born" (absolute) are both Advaita statements. They don't contradict — they speak from different levels. Most confusion comes from mixing them.

## How to use these notes

- Read in order. Each file opens with **"Where we are"** and closes with **"Bridge to the next step."**
- Each step has a **check against experience** — Advaita insists on testing claims in your own experience, not just following the logic.
- Each step flags **contested points** honestly — places where Advaita's argument shows possibility rather than proof.
